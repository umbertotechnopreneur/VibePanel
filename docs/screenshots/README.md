# App screenshots

These nine captures were supplied by Umberto Giacobbi on 21 October 2026, before the product rename. The old name inside the captures is original UI, not replacement artwork.

All PNGs have a 1280 × 864 canvas. Studio windows are cropped to that ratio. Display frames are cropped to their screen content and resized to 1280 × 480, with a plain dark backing so all gallery images share one size. A light conventional unsharp mask improves readability after resizing. No generative AI or AI retouching was used.

The images show the running app and its rendered frames. They are not photographs of a physical display. The screenshots retain their original readings, artwork and text.
